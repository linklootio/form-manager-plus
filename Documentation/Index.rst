Form Manager Plus Lite
======================

Organize TYPO3 forms with categories, search and metadata. Supported Core lines
are TYPO3 13.4 and 14.3; PHP 8.2 to 8.4 is required. The Pro add-on depends on
Lite and adds team workflows, bulk actions and transfer.
